PERSONAL PROJECT01 / 04

ResumeMatch AI

A small app I built to compare a resume with a job description. It extracts text from an uploaded resume, checks for relevant skills and uses sentence embeddings to estimate how closely the resume matches the role.

I used Sentence Transformers with the all-MiniLM-L6-v2 model, cosine similarity and Streamlit. I also added recommendations to help point out skills or requirements that may need a closer look.
